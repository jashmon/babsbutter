## Platform

web

## Users

Babs serves health-conscious individuals and families, aged roughly 20-65, who regularly buy premium groceries and want a cleaner butter without giving up taste or indulgence.

## Product Purpose

Babs Butter offers a range of cleaner, better-for-you dairy and plant-based butters that preserve the sensory richness people expect from butter. The website introduces the brand, explains its products and process, and gathers launch interest.

## Positioning

Babs combines traditional butter-making and familiar recipes with modern food science, offering distinct variants for different dietary and health requirements while keeping richness and flavour central.

## Operating Context

Visitors compare butter variants, learn why the ingredients and churn are different, find stockist information, and join the launch waitlist. The current site is a static Astro marketing site deployed on Vercel, with a client-side Web3Forms waitlist integration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static Astro site with no first-party application backend.
- Five product variants are presented: Classic Salted, A2 Butter, Plant-Based, High Protein, and Unsalted Butter.
- The existing navigation, motion system, responsive behavior, and established Babs visual identity must remain intact when adding new routes.
- Product and launch claims must come from supplied content; future work must not invent availability, nutrition, pricing, or retailer claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Voice: warm, friendly, modern, reassuring, confident, and liberal.
- Brand character: fresh, premium, healthy, sensory, indulgent, rich, and fun.
- Official identity assets are supplied in `/Users/jashmondhabekar/Downloads/brandkit_babs/` and documented in `/Users/jashmondhabekar/Downloads/Babs_Butter_Brand_Guide_Version1.pdf`.
- Official typography: Roketto for display, Miller Text Italic for elegant secondary copy, Public Sans for body copy, with Antique Olive Nord and Professor used sparingly as accents.
- The existing primary and secondary palette, Babs logos, brand shapes, illustrations, and macro sensory photography are binding identity assets.

## Evidence on Hand

- Official 32-page Babs Butter brand guide.
- Complete logo, typography, shape, illustration, imagery, and motion kit.
- Existing branded Astro site and product copy.
- Existing testimonials are present in the site data; their real-customer status has not been independently established and must not be expanded into new claims.

## Product Principles

- Make healthier choices feel desirable rather than clinical.
- Lead with sensory richness and clear product utility together.
- Keep decisions easy: visitors should always know where to go next.
- Use confident warmth and playful restraint rather than generic wellness language.
- Preserve trust by distinguishing confirmed product facts from future or placeholder content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

New routes must preserve semantic headings, keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and meaningful image alternatives.
